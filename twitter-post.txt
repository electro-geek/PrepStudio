Built an AI interview prep platform for the @ElevenLabs Hackathon 🎯

The problem: reading about a topic ≠ being able to explain it under pressure.

So I built PrepStudio — where your entire prep is voice-first.

🎧 Audio Lessons — an AI tutor (Nova) teaches each topic to you by voice. You can interrupt and ask questions mid-lesson. Real two-way conversation, not a recording.

🎙 Voice Interviews — an AI interviewer (Alex) asks you technical questions by voice, one at a time. When done, you get a full score report broken down question by question.

No more practicing in your head.
