WHAT IS PREPSTUDIO?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

PrepStudio is your AI-powered study companion that takes you from "I want to
learn this" to "I can actually explain it in an interview."

Tell it what you want to master and how many days you have — it builds your
entire curriculum, teaches you through live voice lessons, helps you write
about what you learned, and then puts you through a real mock interview to
prove you are ready.

Most study tools give you content. PrepStudio gives you a complete loop:
structured learning, active recall through conversation, teaching others
through writing, and high-pressure testing. That four-stage cycle is how
expertise actually forms.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FEATURES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------


  1. AI CURRICULUM BUILDER
  ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
  Stop staring at a blank page wondering where to start. Type in any topic —
  Python, System Design, Machine Learning, anything — and PrepStudio instantly
  generates a structured day-by-day curriculum tailored to your timeline.

  Every day has a clear focus, every lesson has a purpose, and the whole plan
  is laid out so you always know exactly what is next. No guesswork, no wasted
  time figuring out what to study.


  2. VOICE LESSONS WITH NOVA (AI TUTOR)
  ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
  Reading articles alone does not make things stick. PrepStudio pairs every
  lesson with an AI tutor named Nova who actually talks to you.

  Nova does not just read the material back — she opens with a surprising hook,
  explains concepts through vivid analogies, checks in to make sure things are
  landing, and wraps up with three key takeaways. Ask a question mid-lesson and
  she will answer it on the spot.

  It feels like a real one-on-one tutoring session, not a text-to-speech bot.


  3. ARTICLE REFINER
  ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
  After each lesson, jot down whatever you learned — rough notes, bullet
  points, half-formed thoughts. PrepStudio's Article Refiner takes your raw
  writing and transforms it into two things:

    - A publication-ready blog post in clean Markdown
    - A Twitter/X thread you can share immediately

  It also suggests three article ideas per topic to spark your thinking.
  Learning by writing is one of the most effective techniques there is —
  PrepStudio makes it effortless.


  4. VOICE MOCK INTERVIEW WITH ALEX
  ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
  Once you have worked through 80% of your plan, the mock interview unlocks.

  A professional AI interviewer named Alex asks you eight technical questions
  drawn directly from everything you studied — one at a time, no hints, no
  hand-holding. You answer by voice, exactly like a real interview.

  When it is done, PrepStudio evaluates your entire performance and delivers a
  full scorecard.


  5. PERFORMANCE SCORECARD
  ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
  After every mock interview you receive a detailed, honest assessment of how
  you performed:

    - Overall score out of 100
    - Letter grade (A through F)
    - A two-sentence summary of your performance
    - Your three biggest strengths
    - Three specific areas to improve
    - Question-by-question scores and individual feedback

  You do not just feel prepared — you can see exactly where you stand.


  6. PROGRESS TRACKING
  ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
  A visual progress ring on your plan dashboard tracks completion across every
  day and topic at a glance. Topics toggle between incomplete and complete as
  you work through them. The interview gate at 80% is not an obstacle — it is
  the point. It keeps you honest about whether you have actually done the work.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW IT WORKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Step 1  —  Enter a topic and choose how many days you have to study.

  Step 2  —  PrepStudio builds your full curriculum instantly.

  Step 3  —  Work through each day. Read the AI-generated lesson content,
              then launch a live voice session with Nova to really understand it.

  Step 4  —  Write your notes. Let the Article Refiner turn them into a blog
              post and Twitter thread.

  Step 5  —  Hit 80% completion to unlock your mock interview.

  Step 6  —  Have a real voice conversation with Alex. Answer 8 technical
              questions. Get your scorecard.
